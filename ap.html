Measured wind

Latest readings, with the last several hours as a line (dashed line is 10 km/h). Flagged when the wind is from the east (NE to SE) at 10 km/h or more. The airport is the best guide to wind at the spot; the harbor station is sheltered and reads light.

Buoy forecast

The 7-day model forecast for each buoy, the same NOAA forecast Stormsurf charts. In the Strait, model forecasts tend to overcall what reaches the Elwha, so read it as a heads-up and trust the live buoys.
