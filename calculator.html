The odds compare your swell with every Neah Bay hour since 2020 from 230–319° and 1.5 m+, and how often Angeles Point then read 1.0 m+ at 10 s+, weighting the closest height (±0.5 m), period (±2 s) and direction (±7°). The predicted size is the average height Angeles Point reached in those similar swells, in the hours around the swell’s arrival. For today and future dates the swell shown is treated as running all day, and the odds assume it holds. For a past date the swell is filled from the Neah Bay buoy (NDBC 46087): each hour uses the Neah Bay reading from 2–3 hours earlier, matched to the swell’s travel time, and the Angeles Point buoy (46267) row shows what was actually measured. Buoy history comes from this site’s hourly archive of NOAA readings.
