Swell hours at Angeles Point by month

Hours when Angeles Point read 3.9 ft (1.2 m) or more at 10 s or longer, the size and period that usually means surf at the Elwha. This counts swell only, not tide, daylight or wind (the analysis pages below apply those). Hatched months had little or no data.

Findings from the October analysis

  • Direction matters most. Swell has to come from roughly 270–330° to channel down the Strait. Big Neah Bay readings from the southwest often never reach the Elwha.
  • May is the most consistent month once tide, daylight and wind are applied: about 21 rideable hours in a typical year, and never a bad year. February has the most potential but swings widely. December has the most swell, but it falls on dark tides.
  • The flood current brings more size. Checked against NOAA’s current predictions (not buoy drift, which the wind contaminates), matching your experience on the water.
  • Travel time: about 2–3 hours from Neah Bay to Angeles Point, and 4–7 hours from La Perouse Bank, depending on period and direction.
  • Clean NW swells of 5 m+ at Neah Bay are rare: only 8 since 2004, all between mid-November and mid-January.
  • For the 2026–27 El Niño winter (building toward strong, like 2015–16), mid-March through April lines up best for daylight and tide.
