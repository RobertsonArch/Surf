Sources: NOAA National Data Buoy Center standard meteorological history for 46087 (Neah Bay) and 46041 (Cape Elizabeth); Fisheries and Oceans Canada MEDS wave archive for C46206 (La Perouse Bank). Daily values are the highest reading in each UTC day, with the period and direction from that same reading. La Perouse Bank does not measure wave direction. Swell event times are Pacific Standard Time. Heights are significant wave height (the average of the highest third of waves); sets run larger.
