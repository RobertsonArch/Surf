Sources: NOAA National Data Buoy Center standard meteorological files for 46267 (Angeles Point, CDIP 248), 46087 (Neah Bay) and 46041 (Cape Elizabeth). Lines show the highest reading in each hour. Outside peak is the highest reading from 6 hours before the event starts to its end, so the lag column can be negative when the Strait peaked on a later pulse. Neah Bay sits at the mouth of the Strait; Cape Elizabeth is about 150 km south on the open coast and reads larger in most swells, so the Cape share figures run lower than Neah Bay's would have.
