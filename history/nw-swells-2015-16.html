Source: NOAA National Data Buoy Center, station 46087 (Neah Bay) standard meteorological records, reported every 30 minutes. Hours counts each hour with at least one qualifying reading, and an event groups qualifying readings less than 12 hours apart. The buoy sits at the mouth of the Strait of Juan de Fuca, so it reads lower than open-ocean buoys in big southwest swells. True NW means 293° or further north; readings from 281–292° are WNW. Direction is the buoy's mean wave direction, so a NW swell mixed with a southerly wind sea can read further west than it was. No event qualified in April.
