Source: NOAA National Data Buoy Center, station 46087 (Neah Bay) standard meteorological history (2015 and 2016 files). Values are the highest hourly significant wave height each day (UTC), with dominant period and mean wave direction from that same reading. Significant height is the average of the highest third of waves; individual sets run larger. Neah Bay sits partly in the lee of Cape Flattery, so it reads lower than open-coast buoys in big southwest swells.
