Sources: NOAA National Data Buoy Center standard meteorological files for 46087 (Neah Bay) and 46267 (Angeles Point, CDIP 248), with real-time files for October 2026. Each buoy is reduced to its highest reading per clock hour; Neah Bay direction is its mean wave direction. Each Neah Bay hour is matched to Angeles Point after the swell's travel time over the ~91 km between the buoys, using deep-water group speed for the Neah Bay period (about 3 h at 10 s, 2.3 h at 14 s, 2 h at 16 s), and counts as a miss only if Angeles Point stayed under 3.3 ft for the whole hour either side of that expected arrival. Hours where Angeles Point had a gap in that window are skipped. Neah Bay was offline from Dec 2020 to Sep 2022 and from Oct 2023 to Aug 2024, so no events can appear in those spans. Hours counts hours that met all three conditions; an event groups them when less than 12 hours apart.
